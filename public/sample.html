What AdSense RPM Means

RPM stands for revenue per thousand impressions. In simple terms, it shows how much money a website can make for every 1,000 pageviews or impressions, depending on how the metric is being used. It is one of the most useful benchmarks for comparing website monetization across different pages, niches, and countries.

For AdSense publishers, RPM is often more useful than traffic alone because it shows the earning power of each visitor. Two websites can have the same amount of traffic but very different revenue if one attracts higher-value ads or a more profitable audience.

Why Iceland Has Strong Ad Value

Factors That Affect Real Earnings

The $55.2 figure is a benchmark, not a guarantee. Real earnings can vary depending on ad placement, traffic quality, device type, seasonality, keyword intent, and audience engagement.

For example, a legal website with highly targeted search traffic may outperform the benchmark, while a site with broad or low-intent traffic may fall below it. This is why RPM should be used as a planning tool rather than a fixed promise.

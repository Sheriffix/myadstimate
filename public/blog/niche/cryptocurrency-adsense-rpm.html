How to Increase Your Cryptocurrency RPM

You can't move the global average, but you can influence which side of it you land on:

  1. Target traffic from Iceland: it pays 207% more than the global average for this niche specifically.
  2. Write comprehensive, long-form content: Cryptocurrency audiences — especially in higher-value niches — engage more with in-depth material, which increases ad impressions per session.
  3. Place ads in-content, not just in the sidebar: in-content placements consistently see stronger engagement than sidebar-only layouts.
